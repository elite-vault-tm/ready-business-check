Before You Begin

Building a business on Facebook takes more than posting consistently.

Consistency matters, but intention matters too. Your content should help the right people recognize what you do, understand how you can help, and know where to go next.

Facebook can also help you grow by:

  • ✦ Creating your own space where people can continue learning from you
  • ✦ Joining useful conversations where your audience is already active
  • ✦ Building genuine relationships with other entrepreneurs
  • ✦ Developing collaboration and referral relationships
  • ✦ Paying attention to which posts create real business conversations
  • ✦ Giving interested people a clear destination, such as an offer page, landing page, booking link, resource, or community
  • ✦ Creating simple systems so inquiries and opportunities do not get lost
  • ✦ Tracking what happens after someone follows, comments, clicks, or messages

These five questions will help you look beyond how often you post and consider whether your Facebook activity is supporting the business you are trying to build.

One honest rule: Answer based on the business you are running today, not the version currently living in your plans, drafts, or imagination. 😅
